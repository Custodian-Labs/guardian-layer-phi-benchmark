Where the drop concentrates

The average hides a pattern. Almost all of the loss lands on ASQ-PHI — short adversarial clinical queries where one substitution shifts the whole short span set. The other six benchmarks are flat.

Per-benchmark ΔF1. Deeper clay = larger drop; near-white = no change.

What it means

  • ADetectability survives. Every detector that found PHI in the originals finds the surrogate spans too; the loss is noise-level on 6/7 sets.
  • BRanking is intact. Gemma 4 31B leads and the out-of-domain OBI model trails in both conditions — transform doesn't distort cross-model comparison.
  • CLeakage barely moves (+1.7 to +4.1 pts). Surrogates aren't systematically easier to miss than the PHI they replace.
  • DASQ-PHI is the stress case. Its sparse, short queries amplify any single swap — yet the strongest models still hold above 0.55 F1 there.
