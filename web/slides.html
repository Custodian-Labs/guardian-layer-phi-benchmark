03 · The result结果
±2 pts
statistically equivalent to zero与零统计等价
On the 57,112 masked spans, detector recall is 76.1% → 74.9% (−1.2 pts). An equivalence test (TOST, p≈3e-9) proves the change is within ±2 points of no change at all — too small to matter. 在 57,112 个脱敏 span 上,检测器召回率 76.1% → 74.9%(−1.2 个百分点)。等价检验(TOST,p≈3e-9)证明:变化在 ±2 个百分点内与零等价 —— 小到不具实际意义。
